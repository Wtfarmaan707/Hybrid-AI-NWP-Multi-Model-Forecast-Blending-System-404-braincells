Data sources and methodology

Physical NWP
GFS (NOAA) and ICON (DWD), via Open-Meteo
AI model
AIFS (ECMWF), operational since February 2025
Blending method
Inverse-error weighting per zone, lead time, and variable
Extreme thresholds
IMD-aligned: heavy rain 64.5mm/24h, heat wave departure 4.5C

Forecast Explorer

Blended forecast plotted against each individual source model, by station and lead time.

Solid line: inverse-error weighted blend. Dashed lines: each raw source model. The blend does not simply average the three lines; it leans toward whichever model has the better historical track record for this zone, lead time, and variable.
